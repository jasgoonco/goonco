## Short professional / LinkedIn invitation

Help make cancer research easier to find, understand and critically assess.

GoOnco is a free-to-browse, open-source, nonprofit-oriented initiative connecting public oncology research with visual evidence reports and discussion worksheets. We welcome oncologists, primary-care clinicians, nurses, researchers, librarians, statisticians, engineers, privacy specialists, patients and caregivers.

Try one general research question at https://goonco.org/#chat. Then help with one bounded task: check a source, review a chart, test accessibility, improve a data connector or explain what is missing. Clinician-style reports organize diagnosis, prognosis, medications and testing questions for discussion with qualified professionals.

This is an early information-sharing MVP, not medical advice or a clinically validated service. No patient records, cure promises, institutional endorsement or registered-charity status are claimed. Trials listed through public registries are not endorsed or recommended.

Join: https://goonco.org/#collaborate · volunteers@goonco.org

## Who to invite and what to ask

| Community | First contribution | Review output |
|---|---|---|
| Oncology, urology, pathology, primary care, nursing | Assess one report's applicability and clinical language | Claim-to-source review with conflicts and limitations |
| Trialists, biostatisticians, epidemiologists | Check endpoints, absolute effects, missingness and study design | Reproducible calculation and methodological critique |
| Librarians and evidence-synthesis teams | Check citations, corrections and discovery coverage | Search strategy and source-review record |
| Patients and caregivers | Test readability and prepare discussion questions | Usability feedback using fictional inputs only |
| Engineers, designers and translators | Improve one documented issue | Small reviewed pull request with validation |
| Privacy/security specialists | Review isolation and disclosure controls | Private findings to security@goonco.org |
| Academic institutions and sponsors | Offer bounded review time or infrastructure credits | Transparent scope and support terms; no influence over findings |

## Novel ideas and trial opportunities

Ideas involving decoys, traps, immune targeting or other mechanisms must be recorded as hypotheses until supporting evidence and independent review establish otherwise. Submit a public-source research question with citations, model/species or human-study context, mechanism, proposed endpoint, alternative explanation, potential harms, negative evidence and review needs. Do not submit patient records or self-experimentation instructions.

A completed trial is not necessarily successful; recruiting is not an endorsement. A success claim needs an appropriate comparator, prespecified endpoint, effect and uncertainty, harms and peer/qualified review. Participation and eligibility must be discussed directly with the trial team and qualified professionals.

We cannot promise that cancer will remain nonmetastatic to age 90 or that any approach will make humanity cancer-free. Benign tumors and malignant cancers are different categories; the platform must describe each accurately.

## First four weeks (proposed, not scheduled)

1. Invite a small identified review group with a specific task; use each community's posting rules.
2. Collect comprehension, accuracy, accessibility and privacy findings; triage severity privately where needed.
3. Resolve documented issues, obtain independent review where required and publish a correction log.
4. Expand invitations based on verified usefulness. Track actual reviews and accepted fixes; never invent member or clinical-impact figures.

No bulk mailing, patient targeting, promotional tracking or vendor affiliation is authorized by this kit. Sending drafts requires an owner-selected recipient/channel and an appropriate sender.
