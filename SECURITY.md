# Private security reporting

Send a minimal non-sensitive description to security@goonco.org. Do not post vulnerabilities, secrets or personal/medical data in public issues. Do not attach real patient records or exploit live users. Use synthetic examples; no unauthorized testing, social engineering or denial of service.

Incoming forwarding is owner-tested. There is no staffed response SLA, encryption key, bounty promise or guaranteed remediation timeline. Keep sensitive evidence out of ordinary email; agree a suitable channel before exchanging it. Maintainers should acknowledge, scope, contain, fix, verify and document corrections without revealing reporter details.

Public mode is information-only. /review routes retain owner/platform/PIN checks; member registration and patient storage do not exist. Query limits are isolate-local safeguards, not distributed rate-limit guarantees. Monitor upstream errors and usage before expanding traffic. Hosting operators process operational metadata under their policies.
