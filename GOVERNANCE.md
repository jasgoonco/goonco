# Open participation and accountable review

GoOnco is a community-led nonprofit-oriented initiative, not a verified registered charity or clinical service. Public software and browsing do not make patient information public.

The current repository owner maintains releases and coordinates proposals; this grants no automatic clinical, statistical or privacy expertise. Actual qualified review appointments and source/dataset approvals are zero. Self-selected personas, fictional reviewer fixtures and AI tools have no approval authority.

Propose → scope and conflicts → implementation/evidence → automated checks → maintainer review → required independent qualified reviews → release. Operational source control review does not substitute for scientific/licensing/privacy decisions. Maintainers must not treat an automated pass or model consensus as clinical validation. Preserve negative findings and corrections.

External APIs are technical descriptive connections only. Real datasets require source-specific rights, purpose, version/provenance, scientific/privacy review, expiry and revocation. Publication and reuse/training are separate decisions. No patient uploads, individual recommendations or real-data knowledge updates are enabled.

Review incidents privately, contain/remove affected content as appropriate, retain a correction record, assess dependent views, and re-verify before release. No formal staffed incident-response SLA is established. Contributors may appeal a moderation/maintenance decision to hello@goonco.org; independent appeal reviewers are not yet appointed.

Free browsing does not promise unlimited service or free vendor resources. Program applications must use verifiable status and separate GoOnco accounts/projects; no business data or credentials are reused.
