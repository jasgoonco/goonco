# Community conduct

Treat contributors and people affected by cancer with respect. Welcome different skills, languages and lived perspectives. No harassment, discrimination, coercive fundraising, commercial spam, invented credentials or unsupported cure claims. Do not solicit or publish private medical information.

Discuss evidence and code, not personal attacks. Disclose relevant conflicts. Respect attribution, licensing and consent. Report conduct concerns privately to hello@goonco.org; privacy/security concerns use the dedicated addresses. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or request corrections. Formal independent moderation and appeal roles remain unfilled; do not imply a guaranteed response time.
